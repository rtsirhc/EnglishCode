Your classroom, ready to play.

  1. Find your game

    Filter by CEFR English level, skill or game category. Choose the level that fits your learners.

  2. Make it yours

    Check the instructions, classroom setup, and supported devices. Where available, bring your own JSON content pack or use the built-in material.

  3. Play together

    Open the game on your classroom screen or device. A game’s setup explains whether it needs extra devices or an internet connection.

  4. Keep your favourites

    Save games to your collection. Games marked offline-ready can be downloaded here before class. Settings, packs, and saved games stay in this browser; clearing browser storage removes them.

No account is needed. Downloads and saved content belong to this device and do not sync automatically.

Offline & storage

Download an offline-ready game while connected. Wait for the download to finish before going offline. Online multiplayer and external services still need a connection.

Your browser may reclaim cached downloads when storage is low. Opening the app and checking downloads before class is recommended.
