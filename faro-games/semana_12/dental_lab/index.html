Keyboard: ←/→ slides · Space action/reveal · R replay · H hide word · P pause motion · F fullscreen · T this guide. Drag the model gently to turn it.

Objects you are bringing

Wax model: hold it beside the animation. Ask “One tooth or many teeth?”, “Can you point to the gums?” and “Where would you brush?” Use only as a demonstration. Use the illustrated X-ray on screen and ask “What can you see?”

Pronunciation and meaning

tooth /tuːθ/ → teeth /tiːθ/: tongue gently between the teeth, no /t/ or /s/. gums /ɡʌmz/, final /z/ · cavity /ˈkævəti/ · filling /ˈfɪlɪŋ/, no final /g/ · braces /ˈbreɪsɪz/, two syllables · floss /flɑːs/ · drill /drɪl/ · X-ray /ˈeks reɪ/.

Drill, not mill: dental drill = taladro o pieza de mano. Drill (verb) = perforar. Polish = pulir. Filling = empaste/resina. Fill (verb) = rellenar. Cleaning = limpieza. The animation is a simplified illustration for English vocabulary, not a treatment demonstration. Brushing does not repair an existing cavity.

Drawing task

Give 3 minutes to draw one tooth, not a full mouth. They choose three vocabulary clues, then add their level’s sentences. 2 minutes to write, 2 to describe to a neighbour, 1 to identify and correct. A cavity and braces are optional features, not a diagnosis. If someone prefers not to draw in a notebook, use scrap paper or a verbal description of the projected example. Judge the English, not drawing skill. For a seated group, give each partner a fixed A/B turn.
